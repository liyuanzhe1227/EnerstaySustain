Applicable criteria and reference map

Standard names are shown as text because standards-owner marks do not themselves evidence certification, authorization or endorsement.

Engagement criteriaISO 14064-1:2018 / ISO 14064-3:2019

Organizational inventories and GHG validation / verification. The applicable part and edition are confirmed in the engagement scope.

Engagement criteriaISO 14067:2018 / ISO 14040:2006 / ISO 14044:2006

Product carbon footprints and LCA principles, boundaries, allocation, quality and critical-review requirements.

Engagement criteriaISO 14068-1:2023 / PAS 2060:2014

Carbon-neutrality claims and transition evidence. PAS 2060 is retained only where the specific legacy or transition context requires it.

Audit standardsISO 14001:2026 / ISO 45001:2018+A1:2024 / ISO 50001:2018

Environmental, occupational health and safety, and energy management-system second/third-party audits, subject to the agreed audit criteria.

Assurance standardsISAE 3000 (Revised) / ISSA 5000

ISAE 3000 for applicable non-financial assurance engagements; ISSA 5000 for sustainability assurance as adopted and effective in the relevant jurisdiction.

Disclosure referencesIFRS S1 / IFRS S2 / SASB / GRI / CDP / TNFD

Reporting and disclosure references only. Their use does not mean certification, validation or endorsement by the framework owner.

Accounting referencesGHG Protocol Corporate / Scope 2 / Scope 3

Accounting references used where the reporting programme or client mandate requires them; assurance criteria are separately agreed.

Programme rulesVCS / Verra / Gold Standard

Programme methodologies and rules. Formal validation / verification is undertaken only within the programme's approved entity and competence scope.

Guidance / trainingIWA 42:2022 / IWA 48:2024

Net-zero and ESG implementation guidance used for learning and pathway design; these documents are not certification standards.

Boundary note

Engagement criteria, programme rules and reporting references are identified separately in the contract and report. A name or mark on this page does not imply endorsement of Enerstay. Formal conclusions are issued only through the required authorized or accredited route and within confirmed competence, independence and scope.
