Pilot Scheme

Evidence-led recognition with controlled approval

ENERSTAY TRUSTMARK™ is being prepared as a private scheme for defined sustainability claims and professional competence. Its design separates assessment, technical review and approval decisions.

Current statusGovernance review and pilot preparation
TRUSTMARK™Pilot scheme in preparation

Assessment Scope

Four pathways with defined evidence

Each application is reviewed against its approved subject matter, boundary, evidence requirements and intended claim.

01

Personnel

Training or equivalent experience, examination, case work and continuing competence.

02

Product

Product carbon footprint, life-cycle data, material traceability and supporting test evidence.

03

Organization

GHG inventories, energy procurement, supplier due diligence, governance and sustainability evidence.

04

Project

Project design, baseline and monitoring methodology, independent MRV and milestone evidence.

Important status statement

This page describes a scheme in preparation. It does not announce an issued certificate, government accreditation, ISO certification, or authorization by an external standards body. Pilot eligibility and final mark-use rules will be confirmed before applications open.
