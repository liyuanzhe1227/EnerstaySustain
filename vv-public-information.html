Validation and Verification: Public Information

01

Activities and applicable programmes

Enerstay provides organisation-level GHG verification and product carbon footprint verification, subject to documented competence, impartiality and engagement acceptance.

Organisation-level GHG verification: ISO 14064-1 for the GHG inventory and ISO 14064-3 for verification, together with the agreed reporting criteria and applicable programme requirements.

Product carbon footprint verification: ISO 14067 and applicable product category rules for quantification, with verification under ISO 14064-3 and the agreed programme requirements.

The applicable programme, criteria, scope, reporting period, intended users, assurance level and materiality are agreed before acceptance. Reference to a standard or programme does not itself mean accreditation or programme approval. Regulatory and other programme-specific services are undertaken only where the required recognition and competence are in place.

02

Validation and verification process

1. Application and acceptance: review the claim, engagement type, criteria, scope, resources, competence and impartiality, then agree binding terms.

2. Planning: confirm the assurance level and materiality; perform strategic and risk analysis; plan sampling, evidence and site activities.

3. Execution: examine records and controls, interview relevant personnel, test data and calculations and conduct site work as required.

4. Findings: communicate identified issues, evaluate responses and obtain sufficient appropriate evidence for the proposed conclusion.

5. Independent review and decision: eligible personnel who did not perform the relevant validation or verification work review the evidence and make the decision. Review and decision may be combined only where the applicable requirements permit and competence and independence are established.

6. Issue and follow-up: issue the scoped opinion and related report only after approval; retain records and address facts discovered after issue. Programme-specific requirements take precedence.

03

Commitment to impartiality

Enerstay recognises that impartiality is essential to credible validation and verification. We are committed to objective, evidence-based conclusions and do not allow commercial, financial or other pressures to compromise them.

Relevant interests and relationships are declared and assessed before acceptance and assignment and whenever circumstances change. Conflicts are managed through documented safeguards, role separation, reassignment or declining the affected work. The impartiality oversight mechanism considers relevant interests and reviews threats and safeguards under documented internal SOPs.

Enerstay does not provide consultancy on a claim that it validates or verifies. Fees are not contingent on a favourable conclusion. Independent review and decision are protected from those who performed the relevant work.

04

Complaints and appeals

Any interested party may complain about Enerstay’s validation or verification activities. A client may appeal a validation or verification decision or opinion. These descriptions are publicly available without requiring a contract or account.

Send the matter and supporting information to enquiries@enerstaysustainability.com. We record and acknowledge receipt, confirm whether it falls within our responsibility, obtain the relevant information and investigate or evaluate it under the applicable internal SOP.

The response and outcome are reviewed or approved by eligible personnel independent of the matter concerned, in accordance with the applicable requirements. We communicate progress and the formal outcome, record any correction or corrective action and protect confidential information. Submitting a complaint or appeal does not result in discrimination.

05

Use of our validation and verification opinions

When a client or any other party makes public a validation or verification opinion issued by Enerstay Sustainability Pte. Ltd., or a report of factual findings, it must be communicated in its entirety, together with the statement it relates to and any annexes needed to understand its scope, qualifications and limitations.

Extracts, partial quotations or summaries that could change its meaning are not permitted. The opinion must not be applied to any statement, period, site or product outside its scope. Any reference to our work must identify the statement validated or verified, the standard or programme applied, the reporting period, the level of assurance where applicable, and Enerstay as the body.

An opinion that has been withdrawn or superseded must not be presented or relied upon as current. Any retained historical copy must clearly identify its withdrawn or superseded status and must not mislead users.

The Enerstay corporate logo is not a product or management-system certification mark. Separate client use requires prior written permission specifying the artwork, purpose, medium, scope and period. Permission does not transfer any right to use SAC or IAF marks.

The SAC accreditation mark may be used only after accreditation is granted, within the granted scope and in accordance with the applicable mark rules. Clients must not extract an accreditation mark from a statement or imply that SAC has accredited the client or approved its products or services.

If we identify a misquotation or misleading use, we will require correction or cessation, verify the response and, where necessary, correct the public record and notify the relevant parties, in accordance with applicable requirements and confidentiality obligations.
