Standards & Methods Scope

Standards, specifications and conformity assessment

JEACA welcomes manuscripts that substantively engage with recognised standards, specifications, codes, test methods, technical guidelines, certification or verification schemes, regulatory requirements or implementation frameworks.

Broad by design: no single standards body, jurisdiction or discipline defines the journal. ISO/IEC, PAS/BS, ASTM, GB/GB/T, EN and sector standards are examples only—not an exhaustive list.

01GLOBAL / INTERNATIONAL

International and cross-border standards

International standards, harmonised approaches and cross-border implementation, including—but not limited to—ISO and IEC families.

02NATIONAL / REGIONAL / SECTOR

National, regional and sector frameworks

Standards and codes issued by national, regional, professional and industry bodies, including PAS/BS, ASTM, GB/GB/T, EN and other recognised systems.

03ASSURANCE / CONFORMITY

Assurance, audit, certification and verification

Conformity assessment, accreditation, inspection, testing, validation, verification, certification, audit evidence and governance of trustworthy claims.

04ENGINEERING / TESTING

Engineering, testing and technical methods

Methods, protocols and performance requirements across environmental, quality, safety, energy, materials, infrastructure, manufacturing and digital systems.

05EMERGING / INTERDISCIPLINARY

Emerging and interdisciplinary practice

New or evolving standards, regulatory methods, industry schemes and implementation frameworks are welcome when the normative basis, evidence boundary, limitations and practical contribution are clear.

Editorial & Peer Review

A decision trail that can be audited

The founding workflow separates administrative checks, editorial scope decisions, independent review and final acceptance. Editorial-board appointments will be published only after written acceptance and conflict disclosure.

  1. 01

    Submission and integrity screening

    Scope, completeness, originality indicators, authorship, conflicts, funding, ethics and data statements.

  2. 02

    Editorial assessment

    Methodological fit, contribution, reviewer expertise and any independence safeguards are recorded.

  3. 03

    Independent peer review

    Research, review and methods papers will normally receive double-anonymised review by at least two suitable reviewers.

  4. 04

    Revision and decision

    Author responses, editorial reasoning, conflicts and the final decision are retained in the journal record.

  5. 05

    Production and preservation

    Accepted content receives structured metadata, version control, citation information and a long-term preservation route before publication.

Publication Policies

Public rules for every manuscript

These policies govern the current issue record and will continue to be maintained as version-controlled documents as journal operations develop.

Publication ethics and misconduct

Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, duplicate publication, citation manipulation and undisclosed third-party involvement are prohibited. Concerns will be documented, investigated and corrected using a proportionate process.

Authorship, contribution and conflicts

Authors must identify substantive contributions, the corresponding author, funding and relevant financial or non-financial conflicts. Gift, guest and ghost authorship are not accepted.

AI-assisted work

AI tools cannot be authors or reviewers. Authors must disclose material AI assistance and remain responsible for accuracy, originality, confidentiality and citations. Reviewers may not upload confidential manuscripts to public AI systems.

Data, methods and research ethics

Submissions must explain data provenance, access limits, analytical methods and applicable approvals. Data and code should be shared where lawful and ethical, or restrictions must be justified.

Corrections, retractions and versions

Material errors will be addressed through linked corrections, retractions or expressions of concern. Published versions and dates will remain traceable.

Complaints, appeals and independence

Authors and readers may raise a documented concern. Appeals address process or factual error, not simple disagreement with a scholarly judgement. Conflicted editors will be replaced.

Reader access, licensing and fees

Each article has a dedicated record page containing its abstract, keywords and current access options. Copyright, redistribution and reuse remain subject to the applicable publication terms.

Privacy and review records

Personal data and confidential manuscripts will be limited to legitimate editorial use. Review and decision records will be retained under a controlled access and retention schedule.
