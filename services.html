V/V Process

A defined process from application to decision

Every engagement is screened for competence, impartiality, scope and applicable criteria before contract. This documented sequence applies to V/V work; programme-specific requirements take precedence.

  1. 01Application & contract reviewConfirm scope, criteria, competence, impartiality, resources and terms before acceptance.
  2. 02Plan the workSet materiality, risk, sampling, evidence needs, team roles and the V/V plan.
  3. 03Conduct V/VReview records, data flows and controls; perform testing and site visits as required.
  4. 04Findings & independent reviewRecord findings and responses; an independent reviewer checks evidence, limitations and the proposed conclusion.
  5. 05Decision & statementAn authorized decision-maker approves the result; issue the scoped V/V statement and report, then retain records.

Governance & Due Process

Impartiality, appeals and complaints

Enerstay maintains and applies documented internal SOPs for impartiality, appeals and complaints in validation and verification (V/V) activities, protecting objectivity and ensuring fair, confidential handling.

01

Impartiality and conflicts of interest

Our SOP requires risks to objectivity and relevant interests to be declared and assessed. Conflicts are managed through safeguards such as role separation, reassignment or withdrawal; conclusions are based on objective evidence and applicable criteria, without undue influence.

02

Appeals

A client may submit a written appeal requesting reconsideration of a V/V decision or opinion. It is recorded and reviewed by personnel independent of the challenged activity; its status and outcome are communicated, and resulting actions are recorded.

03

Complaints

Any interested party may complain about Enerstay's V/V activities. A complaint is distinct from an appeal seeking reconsideration of a V/V decision. It is investigated impartially by personnel independent of the subject activity, with confidentiality protected and outcomes communicated.

These process descriptions are publicly available to any interested party. To submit an appeal or complaint, email

enquiries@enerstaysustainability.com
