Evidence Workflow

From source evidence to an independent conclusion

Testing and measurement records may originate from the client or a named technical provider. Enerstay evaluates only the evidence and criteria within the confirmed assignment scope.

  1. 01Define scope and criteria
  2. 02Review measurement evidence
  3. 03Evaluate calculations and control evidence
  4. 04Address findings and complete traceability
  5. 05Issue the scoped conclusion
