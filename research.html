Knowledge System

From published papers to teachable practice

Enerstay Sustainability Academy classifies research by SDG target and ESG use case, then converts selected findings into lecture materials, topic maps, data exercises, discussion notes and publication-training pathways.

01

SDG classification

Each paper is mapped to practical SDG clusters such as clean energy, sustainable cities, responsible production, climate action, quality education and industry innovation.

02

ESG practice notes

Research outputs are summarized into ESG data, MRV, Scope 3, LCA, disclosure analytics, industrial abatement and training-use cases.

03

Publication training

Academy materials can support topic framing, literature mapping, method selection, result interpretation and reviewer-response training.

04

Boundary clarity

Knowledge products are educational and research-oriented. They do not replace formal verification, validation, audit or assurance conclusions.
