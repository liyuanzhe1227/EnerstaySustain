Privacy Notice

This notice explains how personal data submitted through the Enerstay website is collected, used, protected and retained.

Last updated: 27 August 2026

01

Information we collect

Depending on the form or contact channel, we may receive your name, business email, telephone number, organization, requested service or programme, preferred meeting time, project background, publication order details and any information you choose to include.

02

How we use it

We use this information to respond to and route enquiries, assess the requested scope, coordinate meetings or programmes, process publication orders, maintain appropriate business records and protect the security and integrity of our communications.

03

Form and communication providers

Website submissions may be transmitted through the form-delivery provider used by this static website and through our email systems. We do not ask for card details through the website. External links are governed by the privacy practices of their respective operators.

04

Disclosure and marketing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only where needed to respond to the request, deliver the relevant programme or assignment, comply with law, or protect legitimate business and security interests. We do not add you to promotional mailing lists without a separate basis or consent.

05

Protection and retention

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Personal data is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, business records, contractual requirements or applicable legal obligations, then securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

06

Access, correction and withdrawal

You may ask about the personal data we hold, request correction, or withdraw consent where applicable. Withdrawal may affect our ability to continue responding to or delivering the requested service.

07

Privacy contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: enquiries@enerstaysustainability.com.
